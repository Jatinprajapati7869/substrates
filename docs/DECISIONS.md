### D7 entitlement table (from the pricing page)

| Plan                 | Price       | Sections                              | Templates                                                                               |
| -------------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Free account         | $0          | Free sections only                    | None                                                                                    |
| Sections             | $15 / month | Every Pro section, including new ones | 1 template credit per paid month                                                        |
| Sections + Templates | $39 / month | Every Pro section                     | 3 template credits per paid month; choose Webflow or Framer; Figma file where available |
| Lifetime             | $499 once   | Every Pro section, now and future     | Every template unlocked, now and future; Figma where available                          |

Rules shown on the page:

- A credit unlocks one template and all its formats **permanently**. Unlocks survive cancellation.
- Credits reset each month and **do not roll over**.
- The user is always asked to confirm before a credit is spent.
- Full refund within 14 days, no questions asked.
- Video skills are a separate product: $9.99 per month or $79.99 per year, on `/pricing/videos`. Not part of the three plans above.
