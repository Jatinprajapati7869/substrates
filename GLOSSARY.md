# Glossary

Domain language for Substrates. No implementation detail here.

## Credit

A monthly grant that unlocks one template permanently. Credits reset each billing period and do not roll over. Spending a credit creates an **Unlock**.

## Full rebuild prompt

The licensed deliverable for an entitled Pro section or unlocked template: canonical product body plus a short per-assistant wrapper, returned only after server-side entitlement checks.

## Reference prompt

A short, client-safe instruction to recreate a **free section** from its public product page. Not licensed source code and not stored in **Product content**.

## Unlock

Permanent access to one template’s deliverables (all assistant formats in the prompt). Survives subscription cancellation. Created by spending a **Credit** or by **Lifetime** membership.

## Video skills membership

Paid access to premium Claude video skills (style packs, install bundles, and render workflows). Separate from template and section plans. Does not grant template credits or Pro section access.

## Product content

The canonical markdown rebuild body for one catalog product, identified by slug. Not part of the public catalog export.

## Member

A signed-in visitor with a Firebase account. Free sections require a Member; paid deliverables require an active plan and/or **Unlock** as defined in D7.
